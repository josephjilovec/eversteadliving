# Everstead Living

Home accessibility & adaptation planning. An early-stage venture from Joseph Jilovec Venture Studio.

## Positioning

Keep the place you love. Make everyday life work better. A thoughtful planning concept for homeowners and families considering practical, comfortable changes at home.

### Initial audience

- **Homeowners planning ahead:** People who want to understand access and comfort before a move, renovation, or change in household needs.
- **Families supporting a loved one:** Adult children and caregivers who need a shared set of priorities instead of disconnected advice.
- **Local home specialists:** Occupational therapists, accessibility designers and home-modification contractors interested in a clearer client handoff.

## Proposed first offer

**The Home Adaptation Brief** — A proposed, focused planning engagement for one home. The purpose is to prepare better decisions before commissioning design or construction.

- **Routine & room map:** Document entry, circulation, bathing, lighting and daily activities through a guided discussion.
- **Prioritized action list:** Separate everyday adjustments from projects that need assessment, design, or trade expertise.
- **Professional handoff:** Prepare questions, relevant room notes and a scope outline for independently engaged specialists.

## Proposed validation milestones

- **Listen before designing:** Proposed first cohort: interview 10 households and 3 home-accessibility professionals to identify repeated planning problems.
- **Test one complete handoff:** Trial the brief with 5 volunteer households in one local service area, subject to partner availability.
- **Measure usefulness:** Check whether priorities are understood, a professional can act on the brief, and follow-up questions decrease. These are validation goals, not reported results.

These are planning targets, not achieved results. Availability, scope, partners and commercial terms have not been validated or established.

## Website

- Buildless HTML/CSS/JavaScript; serve this repository root with any static server.
- Responsive navigation, native FAQ disclosures, and a browser-local inquiry builder.
- No submissions, analytics, tracking, payments, accounts, API integrations or persistent storage.
- The inquiry builder creates an editable brief. Copy it or open an email draft; the visitor sends it themselves.
- Keyboard navigation, form labels, focus outlines, skip link and reduced-motion support.
- Hero raster asset is AI-generated concept imagery, optimized as WebP. It is not documentary evidence of a site, service or product. See `assets/IMAGE_NOTES.md`.
